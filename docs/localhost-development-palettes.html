localhost development — palette options

Six palettes. Pick one, not a mix.

Each option below keeps the same rules from the brand guide: no tech blue, no purple, no gradients; one warm neutral for backgrounds, one near-black for text, one primary accent for buttons, one secondary for structure, one spice colour used sparingly. The site preview is rendered live in each palette so you compare the feeling, not the swatches.

Contrast figures are text-on-background ratios: 4.5+ passes for body text, 3+ for large text and UI elements only. "Spice" colours are deliberately allowed to fail — they are never used for text.

How to choose

  • Open this page on a phone and on a big screen. Palettes shift a lot between the two.
  • Put your best photo of the five of you next to each preview. The palette that makes the photo look like it belongs there is the one.
  • Pick one. A and D are close enough that mixing them will look like a mistake; the same goes for B and E.
  • Whatever you pick, the rule from the brand guide stays: the copper/brass/amber spice colour appears once per screen at most.
